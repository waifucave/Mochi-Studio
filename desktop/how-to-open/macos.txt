Mochi Studio for macOS: how to open it
======================================

This build is not signed or notarized. macOS (Gatekeeper) blocks apps from
unidentified developers the first time; the warning does not mean anything
harmful was found. It runs on Macs with Apple silicon (M1 and later) and
macOS 13 or later, not on Intel Macs.

1. Unzip Mochi-Studio-<version>-macos-arm64.zip and move "Mochi Studio" to the
   Applications folder.
2. Right-click (or Control-click) the app and choose "Open", then "Open"
   again in the dialog. You only need to do this once.
   - On macOS 15 and later, if there is no "Open" button: try to open the
     app once, then go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down
     and click "Open Anyway" next to Mochi Studio.
   - Or, in Terminal:
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Mochi Studio.app"
3. The first time you start the camera or microphone, macOS asks for
   access: choose "Allow". To change it later: System Settings > Privacy &
   Security > Camera (or Microphone).

More: docs/desktop.md and docs/user-guide.md in the source repository.
