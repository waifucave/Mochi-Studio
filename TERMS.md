## 2. Collaboration: what goes to the other participants

When you take part in a collaboration session:

- **Video only is the default.** The other participants receive a video of
  your avatar. Your model's files stay on your computer.
- **Sharing a model is your choice, per model.** If you choose **its files
  (shared model)**, the model's files are sent to the other participants of
  that session, encrypted, and their apps keep them in memory only and
  delete them when you leave or the session ends. Someone with the right
  tools could still copy the files while the session runs. Choose this only
  for models whose license allows you to share them, and only with people
  you trust.
- **What you send is yours to answer for:** your avatar, its movements,
  your nickname and your chat messages.
- **The signaling server** that introduces the apps to each other is one
  you choose: Mochi Studio sets none, and the Mochi Studio project runs
  none. It is run by whoever hosts it, whose own terms may apply.
