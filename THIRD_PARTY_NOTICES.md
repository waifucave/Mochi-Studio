#### musl

The float32 arctangent, sine and cosine in `src/core/math/libm32.ts` are
ported from musl's `src/math` (`atan2f.c`, `atanf.c`, `sinf.c`, `cosf.c`,
`__sindf.c`, `__cosdf.c`, `__rem_pio2f.c`, `__rem_pio2_large.c`). musl took
them from FreeBSD's msun; their float versions are by Ian Lance Taylor,
Cygnus Support, debugged and optimized by Bruce D. Evans.
